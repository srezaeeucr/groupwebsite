Research Interests

The main research interest in my group is on the observational study of formation and evolution of galaxies using ground- and space-based observatories. This involves both photometric and spectroscopic observation of galaxies. For many years, with my collaborators, I have been involved in designing multi-waveband galaxy surveys. Broadly speaking, my group is involved in the following projects:

Galaxy Evolution & Structure

Study of the Large Scale Structure

Mapping the cosmic web and understanding how galaxies trace the underlying dark matter distribution across vast cosmic volumes.

Galaxy Morphology and Origin of the Hubble Sequence

Investigating how galaxies acquire their diverse shapes and understanding the physical processes that drive morphological evolution.

Scaling Relations in Galaxies

Exploring fundamental relationships between galaxy properties that reveal the physics governing galaxy formation.

High Redshift Universe

Search for Galaxies at the Epoch of Reionization

Pushing the boundaries of observation to find the earliest galaxies that illuminated the young universe.

Nature of Evolved and Massive Galaxies at High Redshifts

Understanding how the most massive galaxies formed and evolved in the early universe.

Search for Rare Populations of Galaxies

Identifying exotic and unusual galaxies that challenge our understanding of galaxy formation.

🔬 Physical Properties & Diagnostics

Statistical Properties of Galaxies

Using large samples to understand the demographics and evolution of galaxy populations.

Spectroscopic Lines Diagnostic of Physical Conditions in Galaxies

Decoding the light from galaxies to measure their chemical composition, star formation, and energetics.

Pixel-scale (Kpc) Properties of Galaxies

Resolving galaxies at kiloparsec scales to understand their internal structure and physics.

🛠️ Surveys & Methods

Multi-waveband Photometric and Spectroscopic Surveys of Galaxies

Designing and conducting comprehensive surveys across the electromagnetic spectrum to build complete pictures of galaxy populations.

Application of AI in Astronomy

Leveraging machine learning and artificial intelligence to analyze large astronomical datasets and discover new phenomena.
